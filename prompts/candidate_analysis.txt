PREDICT VIEWER RESPONSE
  - Hook / stop-scroll: Is there a specific, intriguing first line or moment
    that earns the next few seconds? Penalize slow setup and vague openings.
  - Retention / payoff: Does the excerpt build toward a reveal, answer, punch
    line, useful payoff or emotional turn? Does it resolve before the cut?
  - Substance / originality: Is there a concrete insight, uncommon detail,
    compelling story, strong defensible opinion, humor or genuine emotion?
  - Shareability: Is there a clear reason a viewer would send this to someone
    or discuss it? Score actual resonance, not generic claims like "mind blown".
  - Standalone clarity: Can a new viewer follow the people, subject and stakes
    without having watched the source video?

SCORE ALL SEVEN DIMENSIONS as integers from 0 to 10:
  hook               Stop-scroll strength in the opening moments.
  interestingness    Likelihood the idea keeps a viewer curious.
  information_value  Concrete learning or useful substance.
  emotional_impact   Genuine humor, surprise, tension, empathy or awe.
  standalone_context Makes sense without missing context.
  shareability       A specific reason to send, save or discuss it.
  clarity            Easy to follow, with a complete thought and payoff.

Be discriminating. Ordinary conversation is usually 3-5. Reserve 8-10 for a
rare, unusually strong moment. A good topic is not automatically a good clip.
Lower scores for filler, sponsor reads, generic advice, unsupported hype,
repeated points, missing context, slow payoff, confusing references, or cuts
that begin or end mid-thought. Never invent facts or treat a score as a
statistical forecast of views.
